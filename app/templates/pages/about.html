About the firm

A registered consulting firm with a proven track record.

FC Consulting Engineers was established in 2016 in Malawi and delivers professional engineering and environmental consulting locally and internationally. We are registered with all relevant authorities in Malawi, including the National Construction Industry Council (NCIC).

Our vision

To be a world-class independent consultant in electrical power, energy and environment.

FCCE is a partnership company led by Eng. Mr. Fredrick Munthali and Eng. Dr. Collen Zalengera. The firm is supported by a highly motivated management team and trained technical personnel with expertise in energy, electrical, mechanical, process and environmental work.

Our firm provides technical and economic feasibility studies, engineering design, project management, due-diligence reviews, audits, environmental assessment and professional training — always with sustainability as a design requirement, not an afterthought.
